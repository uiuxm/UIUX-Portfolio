About me

I’m a product driven designer with a strong front-end background, working across branding, product design, and engineering. With 11 Years experience across startups and established brands, I take ideas from concept through to real, shipped products, focusing on how they look, work, and perform.

My strength is in combining design thinking with hands-on development background, allowing me to build and refine ideas while staying within real constraints. This helps me create work that is usable, scalable, and grounded in reality.

I focus on direction over trends. With the pace of new tools and AI, I use it as a co-pilot to move faster while staying in control of the outcome.
